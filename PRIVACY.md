# Privacy Policy — Local Views for YouTube

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Local Views for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- The extension records which YouTube videos you watch on youtube.com (video ID, title, channel name, view count, and first and last view time) to show you your own view counts.
- This data is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking.
- The data is removed when you uninstall the extension.

The `storage` permission is used only to keep these records. The extension runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`.

Questions about this policy can be asked in the project's GitHub issues.

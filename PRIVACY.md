# Privacy Policy — Local Views for YouTube

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Local Views for YouTube does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- The extension records which YouTube videos you watch on youtube.com (video ID, title, channel name, view count, and first and last view time) to show you your own view counts.
- This history is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.
- If you import a Google Takeout file or a backup, the file is read in your browser and its data goes into the same local storage. If you save a backup, the file is saved to your device and nowhere else.
- Your settings (view threshold, counter format and color, where counters are shown) are stored in `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, the browser syncs them across your devices; they contain no viewing history.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking.
- All data is removed when you uninstall the extension. You can also delete your history at any time in the extension's popup.

The `storage` and `unlimitedStorage` permissions are used only to keep these records and settings; `unlimitedStorage` lets a large imported history fit. The extension runs only on `https://www.youtube.com/*`.

Questions about this policy can be asked in the project's GitHub issues.
